## Commercial-launch requirement

Before using this website as an official commercial site, replace the remote mirror URLs with either:

1. owner-supplied originals where Manic Espresso confirms it has commercial web rights; or
2. newly commissioned photography with an appropriate written licence.

Do not silently substitute Google reviewer, Tripadvisor, Wanderlog, Restaurant Guru, competitor or other editorial imagery.
