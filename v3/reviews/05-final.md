# Slice 05 — Final ceramic callback

## Status

**PLANNED — static gate first.**

## Purpose

Close the page with the same tactile ceramic language introduced in the Hero, but in an unmistakably post-breakfast state. The final 3D composition should guide the eye toward the real conversion actions rather than behave like another independent product render.

## Static composition

- off-white ceramic cup and saucer, related to the Hero material language
- visibly lower coffee level
- spoon resting in a different orientation
- small side plate with a few restrained crumbs
- folded receipt/paper element
- warm architectural plinth/table slab
- one restrained floral/stem accent only if it improves balance

The cup sits to the left/lower-left of the CTA area and the spoon/receipt diagonals should point toward **Get directions** / **See the menu**.

## Camera

Desktop target:

- FOV 31–34°
- camera slightly above table height
- controlled three-quarter angle
- enough cup top visible to read the lower coffee level
- no exaggerated perspective

Mobile target:

- tighter crop on cup/saucer/receipt
- remove or crop nonessential secondary props
- HTML CTA remains visually dominant

## Lighting

Use the established upper-left/front-left warm morning key. The scene should feel like the same morning as the Hero, slightly calmer and softer at the end of the visit.

## Static gate

Must pass before motion:

- ceramic, coffee, brass and paper are visually distinct
- cup/saucer/plate feel grounded by contact/cast shadows
- CTA is the dominant semantic layer
- desktop 1600×1000 and mobile 390×844 are composed independently
- WebGL failure leaves a complete HTML CTA and real-photo fallback

## Planned motion after static approval

- very small camera pull-back as the CTA enters
- cup settles by only a few millimetres
- receipt slides/rotates subtly into its resting position
- spoon rotates minimally
- key-light shift is restrained and coherent
- reduced motion holds the approved static frame
